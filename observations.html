Principal Stresses Experiment

What is Measured?

The experiment begins with the given state of plane stress:

  • Normal stress along the xxx-direction, σx\sigma_xσx​
  • Normal stress along the yyy-direction, σy\sigma_yσy​
  • Shear stress, τxy\tau_{xy}τxy​

Using these values, the experiment determines:

  • Principal stresses
  • Principal plane angle
  • Maximum shear stress
  • Tresca equivalent stress
  • Von Mises equivalent stress

Why are the Calculations Required?

These calculations help engineers:

  • Identify the maximum and minimum normal stresses acting on a component.
  • Determine the orientation of the critical planes.
  • Evaluate the likelihood of yielding.
  • Compare different failure theories for ductile materials.
  • Design structural and machine components safely.

Sequential Calculations

Compute the average normal stress

σavg=σx+σy2 \sigma_{avg} = \frac{\sigma_x+\sigma_y}{2} σavg​=2σx​+σy​​

Compute the radius

R=(σx−σy2)2+τxy 2 R = \sqrt{ \left( \frac{\sigma_x-\sigma_y}{2} \right)^2 + \tau_{xy}^{\,2} } R=(2σx​−σy​​)2+τxy2​​

Principal stresses

σ1=σavg+R \sigma_1=\sigma_{avg}+R σ1​=σavg​+R

σ2=σavg−R \sigma_2=\sigma_{avg}-R σ2​=σavg​−R

Principal plane angle

tan⁡2θp=2τxyσx−σy \tan2\theta_p = \frac{2\tau_{xy}} {\sigma_x-\sigma_y} tan2θp​=σx​−σy​2τxy​​

Maximum shear stress

τmax=R \tau_{max}=R τmax​=R

Von Mises stress

σv=σx2+σy2−σxσy+3τxy 2 \sigma_v = \sqrt{ \sigma_x^2+\sigma_y^2-\sigma_x\sigma_y+3\tau_{xy}^{\,2} } σv​=σx2​+σy2​−σx​σy​+3τxy2​​

Tresca stress

σT=σ1−σ2 \sigma_T=\sigma_1-\sigma_2 σT​=σ1​−σ2​

Solved Numerical Example

Given

  • σx=100\sigma_x=100σx​=100 MPa
  • σy=40\sigma_y=40σy​=40 MPa
  • τxy=30\tau_{xy}=30τxy​=30 MPa

Average stress

σavg=100+402=70 MPa \sigma_{avg} = \frac{100+40}{2} = 70\text{ MPa} σavg​=2100+40​=70 MPa

Radius

R=302+302=42.43 MPa R = \sqrt{30^2+30^2} = 42.43\text{ MPa} R=302+302​=42.43 MPa

Principal stresses

σ1=70+42.43=112.43 MPa \sigma_1=70+42.43=112.43\text{ MPa} σ1​=70+42.43=112.43 MPa

σ2=70−42.43=27.57 MPa \sigma_2=70-42.43=27.57\text{ MPa} σ2​=70−42.43=27.57 MPa

Maximum shear stress

τmax=42.43 MPa \tau_{max}=42.43\text{ MPa} τmax​=42.43 MPa

Von Mises stress

σv=1002+402−100(40)+3(30)2=105.36 MPa \sigma_v = \sqrt{100^2+40^2-100(40)+3(30)^2} = 105.36\text{ MPa} σv​=1002+402−100(40)+3(30)2​=105.36 MPa

Interpretation of Results

  • Larger principal stress indicates the most critical tensile stress.
  • Maximum shear stress is used in the Tresca criterion.
  • Von Mises stress predicts yielding based on distortion energy.
  • If the equivalent stress exceeds the material yield strength, yielding is expected.

Result

The experiment determines the principal stresses, principal plane orientation, maximum shear stress, and compares the Tresca and Von Mises failure criteria to evaluate the safety of a component subjected to plane stress.
