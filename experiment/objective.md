After performing this experiment, the learner will be able to:

- Understand the concept of principal stresses in a plane stress condition.
- Determine the principal stresses acting at a point using stress transformation equations.
- Identify the orientation of the principal planes.
- Compare the maximum shear stress criterion (Tresca) and distortion energy criterion (Von Mises).
- Evaluate whether a component is safe against yielding under combined loading.
- Appreciate the importance of principal stress analysis in mechanical and structural design.
